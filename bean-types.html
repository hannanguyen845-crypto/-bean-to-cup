Coffee Bean Types

Explore four major types of coffee beans and their different characteristics. Each type has its own flavor, aroma, and unique qualities.

Arabica

Arabica is one of the most popular coffee bean types in the world. It is often known for its smooth taste and complex flavors.

Depending on where it grows, Arabica may have fruity, floral, sweet, or nutty flavor notes.

Flavor: Smooth, sweet, and aromatic

Robusta

Robusta beans generally have a stronger and more bitter taste than Arabica beans.

Robusta also contains more caffeine. It is commonly used in espresso blends and traditional Vietnamese coffee.

Flavor: Bold, earthy, and strong

Liberica

Liberica is a less common coffee type with larger, irregularly shaped beans.

It is known for distinctive aromas and may have woody, fruity, or smoky flavor characteristics.

Flavor: Woody, fruity, and distinctive

Excelsa

Excelsa is generally classified as a variety within the Liberica coffee species.

It is often associated with tart and fruity flavors. Excelsa can add complexity to coffee blends.

Flavor: Tart, fruity, and layered
